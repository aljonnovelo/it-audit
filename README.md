Objectives:

1. Evaluate the effectiveness of internal controls
2. Identify any weaknesses or vulnerabilities that could compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information

# Features
## Planning & Prep
- Define scope, gather intel, and outline objectives
  - What are the different scopes? COBIT or NIST?

## Risk Assessment
- Prioritize what to test by identifying the biggest threats to your environment
  - What are the common threats to systems?

## Controls Evaluation
- Review access, backups, change management, and more

## Compliance Review
- Compare your policies and practices against legal and industry standards
  - What are the existing legal and industry standards?

## Vulnerability Testing
- Run vulnerability scans/penetration tests and simulate attacks to find weak points
  - What are the common vulnerabilities in systems?
  - How do you simulate attacks to find weak points?

## Reporting
- Receive a roadmap highlighting areas for improvement
  - How do you write a report highlighting areas for improvement?

# Limitations of Audits
## Sampling Limits
- Not every device, process, or configuration is tested

## Point-in-time Snapshot
- Results can become outdated fast in today's fast moving tech landscape

## Reliance on Provided Data
- Introduces the risk of incomplete or inaccurate reporting

## Scope Constraints
- Leave some high-risk areas out of view

## Human Behavior
- It cannot be always be captured ina formal checklist, similar to insider threats
